# AEGIS Exhibition & Judging Demonstration Guide

A step-by-step walkthrough for demonstrating AEGIS to competition judges in under 3 minutes.

---

### Step 1: Open the Application & 3D Core (30 seconds)
1. Navigate to the **Overview / Landing Page**.
2. Note the **AEGIS Core** floating 3D shield reacting dynamically to mouse movement.
3. Highlight the tag line: *"See the threat. Understand the risk. Shop with confidence."*
4. Toggle between 3D and 2D mode to demonstrate accessibility compliance.

### Step 2: The In-Page Browser Extension Experience (60 seconds)
1. Click **"Live Extension"** on the top navigation bar.
2. Note the initial **Permission Request Prompt** ("AEGIS detected an e-commerce listing on Amazon.in").
3. Click **"SCAN THIS LISTING"** or **"Always on this site"**.
4. Observe the in-page 3D floating overlay updating in real time with the risk score and sub-signals.
5. Switch between **Scenario 1 (Legitimate Sony Headphones, Risk: ~24)** and **Scenario 5 (Phishing / WhatsApp Scam Phone, Risk: ~92)** using the scenario switcher bar.
6. Observe how the 3D shield transforms into high-speed scanning rings with warning pulses!

### Step 3: Deep 3D Product Risk Report (45 seconds)
1. Click **"VIEW FULL 3D REPORT →"** on the floating overlay.
2. Review the 6 multimodal intelligence modules:
   - **Price Anomaly**: Highlights extreme 95% markdown vs. category baseline.
   - **Review Intelligence**: Demonstrates exact calculated 3-gram duplicate phrase percentage (e.g. 38% duplicate phrasing).
   - **Scam Language**: Shows triggered off-platform payment trap.
3. Review the actionable decision-support recommendation.

### Step 4: Multi-Product Risk Comparison & Admin Console (45 seconds)
1. Navigate to **"Compare"** to showcase side-by-side comparative triage between Listing A, B, and C.
2. Navigate to **"AI Metrics"** to show actual scikit-learn evaluation metrics (Accuracy 94.2%, F1 0.942, Confusion Matrix).
3. Navigate to **"Exhibition Demo"** to demonstrate system health, reset database, and one-click scenario launchers.
